# Privacy Policy for KidSafe TV

**Effective date: June 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how KidSafe TV ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you and your child use the App. KidSafe TV is a parental-controlled YouTube viewer designed for children under parental supervision.

**This app is directed to children under 13. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation as it applies to children (GDPR-K), and the Google Play Designed for Families program requirements.**

## 2. The short version

- We do not collect any personally identifiable information from you or your child.
- All your data stays on your device.
- We do not show advertisements inside KidSafe TV.
- We do not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- We do not create user accounts.
- YouTube videos are played using the official YouTube embedded player. This is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

## 3. Information we do NOT collect

- We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact information.
- We do not collect device IDs, advertising IDs, or persistent identifiers.
- We do not collect location.
- We do not access photos, contacts, microphone, or camera.
- We do not collect viewing history beyond what is stored on the device for the parent to review.
- We do not require sign-in.

## 4. Information stored locally on your device

For the App to work, certain settings are stored in the App's private storage. This data never leaves your device:

- Parent PIN (one-way salted SHA-256 hash, in the device's secure keystore)
- List of approved YouTube channels
- Individually approved videos
- Liked videos
- Daily watch-time setting and minutes watched today
- Time-left reminder preference

Uninstalling the App removes all of this data.

## 5. YouTube content and Google services

KidSafe TV plays YouTube videos using:

1. The YouTube Data API v3 — for metadata lookup when a parent searches for content
2. The official YouTube embedded IFrame Player — to play videos in-app

When your child watches a video, the embedded player connects to YouTube's servers. Google may collect information about that playback under their own privacy policy. We have configured the player to use youtube-nocookie.com where possible and we filter for "Made for Kids" content.

For details on what Google collects:
- YouTube Kids Privacy Notice: https://kids.youtube.com/t/privacynotice
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Advertising

KidSafe TV's own interface does not display advertising. We do not integrate any advertising SDK.

However, individual YouTube videos may contain advertisements chosen by Google. YouTube's Developer Policies prohibit us from blocking or modifying these ads. Ads on "Made for Kids" content are required by Google to be non-personalized.

## 7. Verifiable parental consent (COPPA)

Because KidSafe TV does not collect any personal information from your child, no separate "verifiable parental consent" is required under COPPA. Your installation and use of the App constitutes your authorization for it to operate as described.

You as the parent retain full control through the PIN-protected Parent Zone:

- See which channels and videos are approved
- Change or remove approved content at any time
- Reset your PIN at any time
- Uninstall the App to delete all local data immediately

## 8. Children's data rights

You have the right to:

- Review any information stored about your child (all visible in Parent Zone)
- Delete any data (uninstall removes everything)
- Refuse further collection (we don't collect data remotely)

## 9. Security

The Parent PIN is stored only as a salted SHA-256 hash in the Android Keystore. The plaintext PIN is never written to disk.

No data is transmitted to KidSafe TV servers because we do not operate any servers. The only network requests are directly to YouTube/Google for content lookup and playback.

## 10. Third-party services

The App makes network calls only to Google-operated endpoints:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/youtube/v3/...` — content searches
- `https://www.youtube.com/embed/...` and `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/...` — playback
- `https://i.ytimg.com/...` — thumbnails

We do not integrate with Facebook, TikTok, or any other social network or analytics platform.

## 11. Data retention

There is no remote data to retain. Local data is retained until you remove it from the Parent Zone or uninstall the App.

## 12. International users

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with data-protection laws:

- The legal basis for any local data storage is your consent by installing and using the App
- We do not transfer data across borders because we do not collect data remotely
- You may exercise your data-protection rights directly through the in-app Parent Zone
